Chapati recipe

Description

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt (optional)
  • 3/4 cup warm water (adjust as needed)
  • 1 tablespoon oil

Steps

  1. Make the Dough
  2. Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rest for 20–30 minutes.
  3. Divide the dough into equal-sized portions (about the size of a golf ball).
  4. Lightly flour your rolling surface and rolling pin. Roll each ball into a thin, round disc about 6–8 inches in diameter. Keep the rolled chapatis covered with a cloth to prevent them from drying out.
  5. Place one rolled chapati on the hot pan. Cook for about 30 seconds or until bubbles start forming on the surface.
  6. Flip the chapati and cook the other side for another 30 seconds. Press gently with a spatula or cloth to ensure even cooking.
  7. Flip the chapati and cook the other side for another 30 seconds. Press gently with a spatula or cloth to ensure even cooking.
  8. Remove from the pan and keep warm in a covered container or wrap in a clean kitchen towel.
