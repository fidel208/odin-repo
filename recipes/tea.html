Tea recipe

Description

Ingredients

  • 1/2 of water
  • Tea leaves
  • Sugar
  • Milk

Steps

  1. Add the 1/2 litre water to an empty sufuria
  2. Wait for like 5 minutes until the water starts boiling
  3. Add 1/2 a cup of tea leaves and wait for like 10 minutes
  4. Add your milk and wait until it boils
  5. Remove the tea from the heating material and put it in a thamos as it awaits serving
